About NU NOCKI GROUP™

NU NOCKI GROUP is an independent record label based in Philadelphia, PA. We sign, record, produce, finish and release music, and we get it to listeners on every major streaming platform. Our founding artist is Jay Gip.

Services

What NU NOCKI GROUP does

Record label services

NU NOCKI GROUP works with artists from first demo to release: artist development, song selection, release planning, artwork and branding, and promotion of each release.

Music distribution

NU NOCKI GROUP distributes music to digital streaming providers, including Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube, on behalf of its artists, and releases it under the NU NOCKI GROUP Presents banner.

Recording

As a record label, NU NOCKI GROUP provides music recording services, running and overseeing sessions to capture the final performances for each record.

Music production

NU NOCKI GROUP provides music production services with its producers, from building the beat and arrangement to shaping the final sound of each song.

Post-production

NU NOCKI GROUP provides post-production services in the field of music, including mixing and mastering, so every release is finished and ready for streaming.

Music videos and content

NU NOCKI GROUP produces official music videos and visual content to support each release.
