Jay Gip™

Rapper and recording artist from Philadelphia, PA. Nu Nocki Group.

Bio

Jay Gip is an independent rapper and recording artist from the Feltonville section of Philadelphia. He has been building his catalog since 2016, from early records like "Critical" (2018) to recent singles like "Fling" (2024), "Finish" (2025) and "Disappear" (2026).

Rather than wait on a deal, Jay Gip founded his own label, Nu Nocki Group, to release his music on his own terms. "Disappear" came out under the Nu Nocki Group Presents banner in 2026, and he is now at work on his album.
